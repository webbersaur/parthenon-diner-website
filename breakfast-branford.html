Breakfast Served All Day

Breakfast in Branford, CT — Served All Day

Start your morning right at Parthenon Diner on East Main Street in Branford, CT, where breakfast is served all day. Just a short drive from Branford Green on Route 1, we've been a favorite breakfast spot for families across Branford, Guilford, East Haven, and North Branford since 1985. From fluffy pancakes and crispy Belgian waffles to classic eggs benedict and hearty omelettes, our breakfast menu has something for every appetite.

Whether you're looking for a quick breakfast sandwich on your commute, a lazy weekend brunch with the family, or an all-day breakfast craving at dinnertime, Parthenon Diner Branford is your go-to breakfast destination on Connecticut's shoreline. We use fresh ingredients and cook everything to order, just like home.
