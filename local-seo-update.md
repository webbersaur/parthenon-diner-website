## Google Business Profile — Weekly Optimization

Your Google Business Profile is one of the most powerful tools for local search visibility. Google's algorithm heavily favors businesses that **actively manage and update their GBP on a regular basis**. A well-optimized website gets you halfway there — but consistent GBP activity is what drives the Local Pack rankings (the map results that appear above organic results).

### Why Weekly GBP Activity Matters

- **Google rewards engagement.** Businesses that regularly update their GBP signal to Google that they are active, relevant, and trustworthy. Inactive profiles get deprioritized over time.
- **The Local Pack is where the clicks are.** For restaurant searches, the 3-pack map results capture the majority of clicks. GBP optimization is the primary way to rank there — website SEO alone won't do it.
- **Competitors are doing it.** If nearby restaurants are posting weekly and responding to reviews while your profile sits idle, Google will favor their listings over yours.

### What to Do Every Week

| Activity | Why It Helps |
|----------|-------------|
| **Post a Google Business update** (photo of a dish, daily special, seasonal item, event) | Fresh posts show Google your business is active and give customers a reason to engage. Posts appear directly on your listing. |
| **Respond to every new review** (positive and negative) | Review responses are a confirmed ranking factor. Thank happy customers by name and address concerns professionally. Even a short "Thank you!" counts. |
| **Upload new photos** (food, interior, staff, exterior) | Listings with 100+ photos get 520% more calls than average. Google uses photo recency as a freshness signal. |
| **Update hours for holidays/special events** | Keeps your listing accurate and avoids "hours might differ" warnings that reduce trust. |


### Monthly GBP Tasks

- **Update the business description** with seasonal keywords (e.g., "outdoor dining" in summer, "holiday catering" in November)
- **Ensure each GBP listing links to its dedicated location page** (`/branford` and `/old-saybrook`) instead of the homepage
- **Review your GBP insights** — track which search terms are driving views and calls
- **Add new menu items or services** as they change
- **Check for and remove duplicate listings** or suggested edits from the public

### The Bottom Line

Think of your GBP as a social media profile for Google Search. The businesses that show up in the Local Pack aren't just the ones with the best websites — they're the ones that consistently interact with their profile. **15-20 minutes per week** of GBP activity can have a bigger impact on local rankings than any single website change.
