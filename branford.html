Our Branford Location

A Branford Landmark Since 1985

Located at 374 East Main Street near Branford Green, Parthenon Diner is a beloved neighborhood restaurant serving the Branford community and Connecticut's shoreline. Whether you're a local or just passing through on Route 1, you'll find a warm welcome and a great meal waiting for you.

We proudly serve guests from Branford, Guilford, East Haven, North Branford, and communities throughout the shoreline. From fluffy pancakes and eggs benedict at breakfast to gyros and souvlaki at dinner, there's something for everyone in the family.
